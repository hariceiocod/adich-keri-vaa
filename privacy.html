Adich Keri Vaa! – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Short version: your photos never leave your device, and we don't collect anything about you.

Photos you upload

When you choose a photo, it is opened only inside your own browser or app. Face detection also runs on your device. The photo is not uploaded, sent, stored on any server, or shared with anyone. It is gone when you close or reload the game.

Information we collect

None. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Things loaded from other services

  • Google Fonts supplies the game's lettering.
  • jsDelivr supplies the face-detection library the first time you upload a photo.
  • Vercel hosts the game.

Like any website, these services may see standard technical details such as your IP address when your device downloads files from them. We do not receive or use this information.

Saved on your device

If you install the game, a copy is saved on your device so it can work offline. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing site data.

Disclaimer
